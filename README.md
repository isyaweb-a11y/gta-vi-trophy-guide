An offline trophy guide and tracker for Grand Theft Auto VI on PlayStation 5: all 55 official trophies with their names, descriptions and grades, a guide to each one in our own words with its sources cited, a roadmap to the platinum, missable trophies flagged, a checklist that saves in your browser with export and import, filters by kind and grade, and a spoiler setting that hides story trophies until you earn them. It is plain static files under `app/` — open `app/index.html` in a browser, no network needed.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
# then open http://localhost:8080
```
